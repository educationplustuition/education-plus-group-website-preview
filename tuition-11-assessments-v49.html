Full Assessments

Best when a learner is ready for a more complete test experience. Full assessments bring the major disciplines together, require sustained focus and produce a broader evidence set for diagnosis and next-step planning.

Part Assessments

Best when a shorter, more manageable assessment is more useful. Part assessments can support focused practice, progress checks and diagnostic snapshots without the demand of a complete full sitting.

What happens after the assessment?

Education Plus assessment design is built around what the evidence can tell us next — not simply the final total.

1

Review

Look beyond correct and incorrect answers to patterns, timing, independence and the way errors occurred.

2

Diagnose

Identify strengths, priority gaps, recurring misconceptions and the strands that need more secure understanding.

3

Explain

Use worked solutions, section diagnostics and clear parent-facing interpretation so the evidence is understandable.

4

Target

Turn the findings into purposeful next steps for tuition, home practice, recovery work, stretch or further assessment.

EP Precision Progress

The assessment system sits inside the wider Education Plus method. Assessment is not the end of the process; it supplies the evidence that helps determine the right route through teaching and practice.
