Why we're different

We do not begin with a worksheet sequence. We begin with the learner.

A score can be useful, but it is only the beginning.

Education Plus Tuition is evidence-led, personalised and adaptive. We look at what is secure, what is getting in the way, what the learner is ready for and which teaching decision is most useful now. As new evidence appears, the plan changes.

That means the lesson is not the whole product. The real value is the informed cycle that connects assessment, teaching, completed work, feedback and next steps.

What that creates

An individual Progress Roadmap

The learner's roadmap is not a static list. It is the working picture of what has been understood, what needs attention next and how the route should adapt as progress becomes visible.

  • Strengths and secure knowledge
  • Priority gaps and misconceptions
  • Teaching targets and next steps
  • Evidence that changes the plan

How tuition works

Teaching and evidence stay connected.

The purpose is not to generate more data. It is to make each piece of evidence useful enough to improve what happens next.

01 · UNDERSTAND

Know the learner.

Start with the child's current position, confidence, prior learning, goals and the evidence already available.

02 · TEACH

Make the next decision count.

Choose explanation, challenge, scaffold and practice because they match the need — not because they are next in a pack.

03 · RESPOND

Change when the evidence changes.

Secure learning moves on. Persistent gaps receive a different approach. The roadmap remains alive.

Built from real experience

Education Plus grew from the reality of education — not from a product idea.

Our work draws on a wealth of practical experience across education, school leadership, tuition, curriculum, intervention, assessment, safeguarding, recruitment and educational development.

That experience has shaped an organisation that looks carefully at the way education works in practice, identifies where things can be done better and develops thoughtful, modern solutions around the needs of pupils, families, schools and education professionals.

We combine established educational knowledge with new ideas, intelligent technology and a willingness to challenge traditional ways of doing things when there is a better way.

Education Plus did not begin with a product and then search for a problem to solve. It developed from years of working directly with children, parents, teachers, school leaders and education professionals — seeing first-hand what works, where frustrations exist and where conventional approaches sometimes fall short.

Whether supporting a pupil who needs carefully targeted tuition, helping a parent understand what their child should learn next, creating technology that makes assessment more meaningful, or designing a staffing system around the realities of a busy school office, the starting point remains the same.

Tradition where it matters. Innovation where it helps.

Good education remains human.

Education Plus respects the expertise, relationships and human judgement that great education has always depended upon.

At the same time, we do not believe that established ways of working should be preserved simply because they are familiar.

Where assessment can become more diagnostic, we improve it. Where tuition can become more precisely targeted, we develop the system to make that possible. Where technology can remove unnecessary administration, we use it. Where parents can be given clearer information about their child's learning, we provide it. Where schools can access staffing more efficiently, we redesign the process around them.

Our approach is not to replace good education with technology. It is to use intelligent systems to make good education even better.
