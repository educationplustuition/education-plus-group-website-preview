How Education Plus works differently

Evidence should change what happens next.

The principle already runs through Education Plus Tuition: understand the learner or problem properly, use evidence intelligently, decide the right next step and adapt rather than force everyone through the same route.

How a tuition route develops

Assess. Understand. Target. Teach. Adapt. Progress.

A score can be useful, but it is only the beginning. Education Plus is built around understanding what the evidence means and using it to shape the next teaching decision.

✓Assessment where usefulUse formal or informal evidence to locate strengths, gaps and misconceptions.
✓Clear targetsPrioritise what matters next rather than simply covering more content.
✓Adaptive teachingChange explanation, scaffold, material or pace when the learner needs it.
✓Progress evidenceLook beyond raw marks to what has become secure and what still needs work.

Questions families often need answered

Before you book anything.

Do we need an assessment first?

Not always. An assessment is useful when it will change the teaching decision. If the need is already clear, a conversation and first lesson may be the better starting point.

Is tuition one-to-one, group or online?

Education Plus can use different delivery formats. The right one depends on age, goal, availability and how the learner works best. The Support Finder captures your preference without treating it as a fixed rule.

What if the existing material does not fit?

The existing Education Plus philosophy is explicit: adapt or create what is needed rather than forcing a learner through material that does not fit.

If you are starting early

Build breadth before speed.

Year 4 and early Year 5 preparation should strengthen underlying maths, English, vocabulary and reasoning while gradually introducing selective-test demands.

If the test is close

Do less, more precisely.

Near the test, the priority shifts toward evidence-led gap closing, pacing, technique, confidence and deciding what not to spend time on.
