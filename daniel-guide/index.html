Important autumn update

Your pot is small for a sunflower this tall. But because the plant is already flowering in October, a huge repot is not the first job. Moving a plant into lots of fresh wet compost late in the year can stress it. First, support it, check the soil and only move it if there is a clear reason.

Seed mission

1
Wait

Let the flower finish. Petals will fade and the back of the head will gradually turn yellow-brown.

2
Check

The seeds should look plump and feel firm. If they are thin or soft, they need longer.

3
Cut with an adult

When the head is mature, cut it with some stem attached. Use secateurs only with adult help.

4
Dry it

Keep the head somewhere dry and airy. Do not seal a damp seed head in plastic.

5
Save next year's seeds

When completely dry, rub out some seeds and store them in a labelled paper envelope in a cool, dry place.
