# EDUCATION PLUS TUITION + EP PATHWAYS — CANONICAL CUSTOMER ESTATE

**Authoritative from:** 12 October 2026  
**Scope:** Education Plus Tuition public/customer estate and Education Plus Pathways public/family estate  
**Purpose:** One polished, concise, interconnected family with one clear job for every page, document and login route.

## 1. Governing principle

Education Plus Tuition and EP Pathways are related but not interchangeable.

- **Education Plus Tuition** is the teaching service.
- **EP Pathways** is the living personalised learning system that evolved from the learner-first way Education Plus Tuition conducts 1:1 teaching.
- **EP Trace** is the prerequisite-tracing intelligence within EP Pathways.
- Continuity comes through shared educational philosophy, language, visual family and navigation — not by forcing Pathways promotion into every Tuition page.

The tuition philosophy came first. Pathways formalises and extends that thinking.

## 2. Canonical public family

### Education Plus Tuition

1. **Tuition Information**
   - Definitive parent/customer explanation of Education Plus Tuition.
   - Includes: who Education Plus Tuition is, how tuition works, age/stage coverage, teaching philosophy, fees, safeguarding/trust, expectations, family working, FAQs and contact routes.
   - Replaces a separate top-level “About Education Plus Tuition” destination.
   - Must not become a Pathways sales page.

2. **Availability**
   - One job: show current regular 1:1 tuition availability clearly.
   - The current/next available slot is the primary state.
   - Week and selected-day views are one integrated booking component.

3. **Bookings**
   - One job: arrange/manage tuition booking actions.
   - Must not duplicate Tuition Information.

### EP Pathways

1. **Pathways Hub**
   - Flagship public showcase.
   - Must demonstrate the intelligence and educational difference, not merely describe features.
   - EP Trace is a centrepiece.
   - Show the living evidence loop: understand → trace → teach/check → evidence → adapt → verify/progress.
   - Core positioning: **Find the gaps. Map the path. Measure the progress.**
   - Supporting proposition: **Not just where a learner is. Why they are there — and what should happen next.**

2. **Pathways Parent Guide**
   - Practical family-use guide after the proposition is understood.
   - Covers access, parent/carer view, evidence interpretation, progress, family actions, documents/messages and what to do next.
   - Must not repeat the Hub’s promotional/explanatory job.

3. **Existing Learner Login**
   - Canonical ordinary learner/family entrance:
     `https://pathways.educationplusgroup.co.uk/tuition-login`
   - Older learner-login routes should redirect here.

## 3. Supporting/onboarding routes

### Start Your Pathway

`https://pathways.educationplusgroup.co.uk/learner-details`

This is an **onboarding/intake action**, not a peer top-level public destination. Surface it contextually when a family is ready to begin or has been invited to provide learner information.

### Pathways Explained
Historical canonical route:
`https://pathways.educationplusgroup.co.uk/guide?start=1`

This is **retired as a top-level customer destination**. Valuable material should be absorbed into:
- Pathways Hub when it explains the educational/intelligence proposition;
- Pathways Parent Guide when it explains practical family use.

The historical URL should remain safe through a compatibility redirect or contextual handoff rather than becoming a dead link.

## 4. Terminology

Use:
- **EP Pathways** / **Pathways** — overall living personalised learning system
- **EP Trace** — prerequisite/tracking-back intelligence
- **Find the gaps. Map the path. Measure the progress.**

Do not use as active product names:
- Progress Roadmap
- EP Progress Pathway
- PathwayTrace
- competing “progress” product labels where they refer to EP Pathways

Legacy terminology may remain only in historical/provenance records where changing it would misrepresent the record.

## 5. Tuition narrative

About/Tuition Information should stand on its own educationally.

Preferred approved language includes:
- **A different kind of tuition.**
- **We don’t begin with assumptions. We begin with the learner.**
- **Teaching first. Systems in support.**
- **More than a lesson. More than a worksheet.**
- **Built from real educational experience.**
- **Tradition where it matters. Innovation where it helps.**
- **Understand the need first. Build the solution around it.**

It may naturally describe the learner-first behaviours from which Pathways evolved — identifying gaps, looking beneath mistakes, tracing foundations where necessary, deciding what matters next, adapting teaching and verifying learning — without repeatedly naming Pathways.

## 6. Pathways Hub quality requirement

The Hub must cause the intelligence to be understood through demonstration.

A visitor should be able to see the difference between:
- recording an incorrect answer; and
- using evidence to investigate **why** the difficulty exists.

A canonical EP Trace demonstration should show:
current difficulty → relevant prerequisite → earlier foundation where required → root insecurity/confirmed secure boundary → targeted response → new evidence → Pathway adapts.

Do not claim that one wrong answer automatically identifies a gap. The system protects secure learning and traces only as far as evidence requires.

The Pathway is **living**:
new assessment evidence, teaching evidence, independent work, learner response and professional verification can change priorities and next steps.

## 7. Visual family

Use approved master assets only. Do not redraw official logo geometry.

The family may use:
- approved Education Plus Tuition Gold master;
- approved Pathways Gold master;
- approved Pathways cinematic artwork;
- corrected full Pathways hero;
- approved EPT roundel/wordmark masters;
- approved gold Pathways vector wordmark;
- existing blue/white formal variants where appropriate.

The gold/cinematic assets are high-value identity moments, not wallpaper. Use them where they add hierarchy, theatre or meaning. Do not repeat them merely because they are available.

Shared family qualities:
- deep navy / royal blue / gold;
- disciplined typography and spacing;
- Intelligence Current as a restrained connective motif;
- high-quality transitions;
- portrait-mobile first;
- no PDF-like page composition;
- no competing duplicate branding.

## 8. NEXA behaviour

NEXA means useful intelligence, not decorative motion.

Public/customer surfaces should:
- surface the next useful action;
- remove controls that are redundant in the current context;
- remember appropriate local context such as selected day;
- keep navigation concise;
- use movement to explain state or continuity;
- preserve orientation/device quality;
- avoid forcing users through information they do not need.

Motion without meaning is not a NEXA feature.

## 9. Documents and publications

Every customer-facing document must have one defined purpose.

Examples:
- **Diagnostic Report:** what the evidence found and what happens next.
- **Parent Guide:** how the family uses Pathways.
- **Learner Pathway:** the learner’s live route and progress.
- **Tuition Information:** the teaching service and customer proposition.
- **Pathways Hub:** the distinctiveness/intelligence of EP Pathways.

If two publications substantially answer the same customer question, merge, retire or reduce one rather than maintaining duplicates.

Where appropriate, documents should provide concise routes back to the relevant live destination. Do not turn every document into a directory.

## 10. Navigation rules

On the principal customer entrance, the two families are visually distinct:

**TUITION**
- Tuition Information
- Availability
- Bookings

**PATHWAYS**
- Pathways Hub
- Pathways Parent Guide
- Existing Learner Login

Contact and Menu are secondary utilities.

Rules:
- No permanent Back control on a root/landing view.
- Back appears contextually inside a subview.
- Do not show a permanent “Availability” action while already on Availability.
- Do not expose separate WhatsApp and Email circles as equal primary navigation; use a compact Contact utility.
- Menu belongs in the header.
- Live date/time/calendar may support booking decisions but must remain visually contained within the header.

## 11. Compatibility and redirects

Do not break links already sent to families.

When a surface is retired:
1. preserve its historical URL;
2. redirect/handoff to the new canonical destination;
3. preserve any essential context where possible.

Canonical ordinary learner entrance remains:
`https://pathways.educationplusgroup.co.uk/tuition-login`

## 12. Change control

This document supersedes older customer-estate instructions when they conflict on:
- public information architecture;
- customer-facing route hierarchy;
- current terminology;
- top-level navigation;
- Pathways Explained status;
- About vs Tuition Information;
- login destination hierarchy.

Older documents remain useful provenance unless explicitly deleted.

Educational evidence rules, learner data, assessment state, bookings/payment state and security behaviour are not to be reset by a visual/navigation reconciliation.

## 13. Quality gate

Before release, review:
- desktop;
- tablet;
- phone landscape;
- phone portrait;
- navigation and return paths;
- old-link compatibility;
- all public login links;
- customer-facing terminology;
- duplicate information;
- approved asset usage;
- real rendered output, not source code alone.

A passing build is not proof of a good customer experience.


## 14. Hosting and deployment policy

The permanent public identity is the Education Plus domain estate, including `educationplusgroup.co.uk` and approved subdomains.

Hosting/deployment providers are infrastructure only and must not dictate the customer-facing architecture.

**Default rule: use a suitable free hosting/deployment option first.**

Vercel must be used only when:
1. there is no suitable free alternative for the required workload; or
2. Vercel provides a materially important capability that available free alternatives cannot provide.

Do not create additional Vercel projects merely for convenience, previews or routine static hosting where a suitable free option exists.

Where a service is migrated away from Vercel, preserve the existing Education Plus public domain/subdomain so customer links do not change.

Before introducing any paid hosting dependency, document:
- why the free alternatives are insufficient;
- what capability requires the paid service;
- the expected ongoing cost/risk;
- the migration/exit route.
